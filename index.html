Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device. DevKit has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Effective date: November 19, 2026  ·  Version: 1.0.0

1Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DevKit – Developer Tools (“DevKit”, “the app”), developed by Afaq Ahmad, handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. DevKit does not require you to sign up or log in, and we do not operate any server that receives your data.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking services.

3Data Stored on Your Device

To work properly, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Your settings (such as dark mode), favorite tools and recently used tools
  • Tool run history (short previews of recent inputs and outputs)
  • Snippets, notes and vault entries that you choose to save in the Library

This data is never sent to us or to any third party. You can delete it at any time from Settings or by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Tip: the Vault is stored locally on your device. Avoid saving highly sensitive credentials in any app that is not designed as a dedicated password manager.

4Offline Tools

Most tools (JSON, encoders and decoders, hashing, JWT decoding, regex, colors, date conversion, code generators and more) run entirely on your device. Text you enter into them is processed locally and is not transmitted.

5Network Tools

Some tools connect to the internet only when you press Run, Send or Fetch. They send just the request you create, directly from your device to the service involved:

  • REST API Tester, HTTP Header Viewer: the URL, headers and body you enter are sent to the server you specify.
  • IP Information Lookup: the IP address you enter (or your own IP, if left empty) is sent to ipwho.is.
  • DNS Lookup: the hostname you enter is resolved using your device’s resolver.
  • Free API Explorer: requests go to public APIs (Open-Meteo, Cat Fact, Chuck Norris API, GitHub, CoinGecko and REST Countries) using the values you enter, such as a city, username or coin name.

These services are operated by third parties and have their own privacy policies. We do not control or receive the data they handle.

6Sharing and Clipboard

The app can copy results to your clipboard or open your device’s share sheet when you tap Copy or Share. What you share, and where, is entirely your choice.

7Permissions

DevKit only needs internet access for the network tools described above. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos or files.

8Children’s Privacy

DevKit is a developer utility and is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9Security

Because your data stays on your device, its protection depends on your device’s own security (screen lock, encryption and updates). We recommend keeping your device protected.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date updated above.

11Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit honexio.tech.
